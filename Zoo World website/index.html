More Than a Zoo — It's a Journey

At Zoo World, we believe that every encounter with nature has the power to inspire. Founded on a passion for wildlife and conservation, we've created a place where families, explorers, and animal lovers of all ages can connect with the natural world like never before.

Top Animals

African Lion

The king of the savanna. Watch our pride of lions bask, play, and roam in our expansive African habitat — a truly breathtaking sight.

African Elephant

The largest land animal on Earth. Witness their intelligence, emotion, and grace up close in our dedicated elephant sanctuary.

Giraffe

Standing up to 6 meters tall, our giraffes are impossible to miss. Join a feeding experience and look them right in the eye.

Snow Leopard

Elusive, powerful, and breathtakingly beautiful. Our snow leopards roam a cold mountain habitat built just for them.

Macow

A living explosion of color. Walk through our free-flight rainforest aviary and let these magnificent parrots take your breath away.

Orangutan

Among our closest relatives in the animal kingdom. Observe their remarkable problem-solving skills and gentle family bonds daily.

Interesting Facts

Otter

Sea otters hold hands while sleeping so they don't drift apart. This group is called a "raft" and can include hundreds of otters.

Lion

A lion's roar can be heard up to 8 km away, and they roar to communicate location and reinforce group bonds.

Hippo

Hippos secrete a natural pinkish-red liquid that acts as both sunscreen and an antimicrobial agent.
